## Communication Style

- Be concise. One sentence where possible.
- State what you did, not what you're about to do.
- Don't narrate your thought process unless asked.
- Don't apologize or hedge.
- Summarize the outcome, changed files, and verification in a few sentences. Show full code, diffs, or command output only when requested.
- Keep progress updates brief and report new findings; do not repeat that a check is still running.
- If you made a mistake, fix it directly.
